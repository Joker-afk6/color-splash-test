Color Splash

The Silent District, its restoration, and everything GREEN: the vine bridge, the Belverde Ascent, the Bloom Run and the Hidden Grotto. Wake the crystal at the heart of the district to awaken GREEN.

Needs a computer with a keyboard and mouse. The first load downloads about 95 MB of world and character models, so give it a minute. Progress saves in this browser; it resumes at your last checkpoint after the crystal is awake. Use your browser's Back button to return here.

Controls

  • WASD Move
  • Mouse Look around (click to capture, Esc to release)
  • Space Jump, again in the air to double jump; at a ledge, climb
  • Shift Dash (hold to sprint)
  • E Interact: wake the crystal, launch from the seed pod
  • F Use the colour (or left click while the mouse is captured)
  • 1–5 Choose a colour; Q next colour
  • R Back to the last checkpoint
  • Esc Skip a cinematic
  • F3 Hide the debug panel
