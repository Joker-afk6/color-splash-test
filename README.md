# Color Splash — playable test build

Compiled game only (no source). Play in a desktop browser (keyboard and mouse) or on a phone or tablet held sideways (touch controls):
**https://joker-afk6.github.io/color-splash-test/**

This build: the cinematic prologue, the whole of Ruby Orchard to "Chapter 1 complete", and the start of Chapter 2,
Whisperwave Bay (New game → choose Tarin or Mira). Continue resumes at the last checkpoint.

New in this build: after Chapter 1, a bubble waits at the orchard's far gate. Step into it to reach **Whisperwave
Bay**: the Coast Road, Gull's Lookout over the bay, and the shell stair down to Shellside. Its first look: the living
sea, weather, wind and waterfalls, all still grey but for red, because blue is lost. The bay's story and Bloop come
next.

To start part-way (the address then becomes ?continue=1):
**…/?at=rescue** (Ruby's rescue), **…/?at=liberation** (the seal just broken: her liberation),
**…/?at=courtyard** (Flame Burst in hand), **…/?at=bay** (the start of Whisperwave Bay) — add **&hero=mira** for Mira.
Controls: WASD move · mouse look (click to capture, Esc releases) · Space jump/climb · Shift dash ·
E tap with the Wonderbrush · F Flame Burst · Enter/Esc skip a cutscene · M music on/off · H controls card.
The first load downloads about 90 MB; the bay adds about 25 MB when you reach it.
