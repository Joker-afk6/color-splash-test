# Color Splash — playable test build

Compiled game only (no source). Play in a desktop browser (keyboard and mouse) or on a phone or tablet held sideways (touch controls):
**https://joker-afk6.github.io/color-splash-test/**

This build: the cinematic prologue and the whole of Ruby Orchard, to "Chapter 1 complete" (New game → choose Tarin
or Mira). Continue resumes at the last checkpoint.

New in this build: Ruby's liberation (red comes back as a wave from her, the Explorer sash, the Ember Dye), Flame
Burst (F, or ◆ on a phone), the courtyard (light the torch, melt three wax seals) and the far gate with the chapter's
end. Earlier: a glowing trail and how-to cards at every step, a black-and-white world until a colour is freed, sharp
cutscenes.

To start part-way (the address then becomes ?continue=1):
**…/?at=rescue** (Ruby's rescue), **…/?at=liberation** (the seal just broken: her liberation),
**…/?at=courtyard** (Flame Burst in hand) — add **&hero=mira** for Mira.
Controls: WASD move · mouse look (click to capture, Esc releases) · Space jump/climb · Shift dash ·
E tap with the Wonderbrush · F Flame Burst · Enter/Esc skip a cutscene · M music on/off · H controls card.
The first load downloads about 90 MB.
